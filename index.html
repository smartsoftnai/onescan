Frequently Asked Questions

Learn more about OneScan, QR code safety, AI-powered scanning, and how the app can help you understand scanned links.

What is OneScan?

OneScan is an AI-powered QR code and barcode scanner for iPhone. It helps you scan QR codes, understand the content behind them, and get additional information about potentially suspicious links before you open them.

Is OneScan a safe QR code scanner?

OneScan is designed to help users make more informed decisions before interacting with QR code links. It can scan QR codes and provide additional information about their contents and potentially suspicious URLs.

Can OneScan check if a QR code link is suspicious?

OneScan can analyze scanned QR code content and provide additional information about potentially suspicious links. The app is designed to give users more context before opening a link.

Does OneScan use AI to analyze QR codes?

Yes. OneScan includes AI-powered analysis that can help explain scanned content and provide useful information about QR code results and links.

Can OneScan scan barcodes?

Yes. OneScan supports QR code and barcode scanning, allowing you to quickly capture and review scanned information.

Does OneScan work on iPhone?

Yes. OneScan is available for iPhone through the Apple App Store.

Is OneScan free to use?

OneScan is available as a free app with optional premium plans. Premium plans provide an ad-free OneScan experience.

Where can I download OneScan?

OneScan is available on the Apple App Store. You can download the app directly from the official OneScan website or through the App Store.
